    About Divisiri

    Where nutrition, innovation, and everyday wellbeing meet.

    Who we are

    Divisiri is an independent wellness and innovation company. We work at the intersection of nutrition science, wellness needs, and product development, translating real consumer needs into thoughtfully developed solutions.

    What we believe

    We believe wellness solutions should be developed with care and communicated with clarity — grounded in genuine consumer need rather than trend-chasing, and reviewed carefully before they reach the market.

    Mission

    To develop thoughtful nutrition, wellness, and personal-care solutions that respond to real consumer needs.

    Vision

    To make responsible, useful, and clearly communicated wellness solutions more accessible across markets.

    Our values

    Scientific curiosity

    We stay curious about nutrition and wellness science.

    Responsible innovation

    We innovate carefully, with consumer wellbeing in mind.

    Consumer empathy

    We start from real, understood needs.

    Transparency

    We aim to communicate clearly about what we do and don't yet know.

    Quality discipline

    Quality review is part of how we work, not an afterthought.

    Long-term thinking

    We build for lasting relevance, not short-term trends.

    Respect for traditional knowledge

    We approach traditional wellness knowledge with respect and care.

    Continuous improvement

    We look to refine our approach as we learn more.

    Who we serve

    Divisiri works with organisations and partners looking to develop nutrition, wellness, nutraceutical, and personal-care concepts — from early-stage exploration through private-label and co-branding collaborations.

    Areas of expertise

    Our areas of focus include medical nutrition, wellness nutrition, nutraceuticals, Ayurvedic-inspired wellness, cosmeceuticals and personal care, and functional food concepts. See our Solutions page for a full breakdown.

    Our commitment to transparency

    Developed with care. Communicated with clarity. We aim to be clear about what is confirmed, what is still under development, and what requires further verification before it is published.
